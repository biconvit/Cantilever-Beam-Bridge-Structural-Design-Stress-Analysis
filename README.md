# Cantilever-Beam-Bridge-Structural-Design-Stress-Analysis
- Redesigned a 500 mm unsupported cantilever bridge deck subject to an 8 kg dynamic moving load under a strict 1-end clamped boundary condition.
- Optimized cross-sectional geometry (incorporating vertical web stiffeners / box-girder profiles) using SolidWorks to maximize the area moment of inertia (I) within a tight 0.3 m² raw material constraint.
- Formulated analytical beam deflection and bending stress models (σ = My/I), achieving a tensile Safety Factor (SF) ≥ 1.5 and keeping maximum cantilever deflection strictly below the 5.0 mm allowable threshold.
- Prepared complete fabrication documentation, including 3D assemblies, Bill of Materials (BOM), and 2D flat patterns (.DXF) for precision CNC laser cutting.
- Executed adhesive solvent bonding without mechanical fasteners and performed physical multi-cycle load tests, validating that empirical deflection closely matched analytical predictions.
